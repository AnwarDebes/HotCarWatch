The only intervention that prevents heat injury with certainty is to never leave any child or pet in any parked vehicle for any length of time.

The model

HotCarWatch uses the standard cabin lumped-capacitance ODE. Cabin air, dashboard, seats, and the inner steel plate are treated as one thermal mass M (J/K). Three flux terms act on the lump:

M dT_c/dt = Q_solar(t) - U*A*(T_c - T_amb) - vent*(T_c - T_amb)

The equilibrium cabin temperature is T_c,eq = T_amb + Q_solar / (U*A + vent), and the time constant is τ = M / (U*A + vent). For a typical sedan, τ ≈ 22 min, matching McLaren et al. 2005 and the NIA tabulated values.

Occupant core temperature

The occupant's core body temperature follows a simplified heat balance driven by the cabin air temperature:

m_body c_body dT_core/dt = Q_metabolic + h*A*(T_cab - T_core) - Q_evap

Sweating (humans) provides evaporative cooling up to ~38 °C cabin air, then derates above that. Dogs cool only by panting (no sweat glands except paw pads), and panting is ineffective above ~40 °C cabin air. Cats and brachycephalic breeds (pugs, bulldogs) thermoregulate even worse.

The data the model is calibrated to

  • McLaren, Null & Quinn 2005 Pediatrics 116:e109-e112 -- cabin rise of ~22 °C above ambient in 1 hour, independent of outdoor temperature; 80% of total rise in first 30 min; cracking windows had negligible effect.
  • Grundstein, Meentemeyer & Dowd 2009 Int J Biometeorol 53:255-261 -- maximum cabin temperatures 41-76 °C in Athens GA summer.
  • Vanos et al. 2018 Temperature 5:276-292 -- Phoenix shade vs sun cabin delta of ~8 °C.
  • Bouchama & Knochel 2002 NEJM 346:1978 -- heat stroke at core 40 °C, cell death and likely fatality at 42 °C.
  • NoHeatstroke.org (Jan Null, San Jose State University) -- 1047 US pediatric vehicular heat-stroke deaths since 1998, average ~37 per year, 54% under age 2.

The data the model is NOT

HotCarWatch is a public-domain physical model. It is not a substitute for common sense, parental judgment, or legal advice. Leaving children alone in vehicles is a criminal offense in many US states; HotCarWatch is for educational use to demonstrate the speed at which cabin and core body temperatures rise.

Privacy

HotCarWatch runs entirely in your browser. When you click “Save my vehicle”, the configuration is stored in your browser's localStorage only.
