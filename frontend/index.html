What "interventions" actually buy

None of these make the car safe. They only delay heat injury.

    HotCarWatch is a public-domain physical model. Do not use it to justify leaving any child or animal in any parked vehicle. In the US, 1047 children have died of vehicular heat stroke since 1998 (NoHeatstroke.org, Jan Null SJSU). In every case, the only safe option was to take the child with you.
